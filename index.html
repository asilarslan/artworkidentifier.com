FAQ

FAQ

Is Artwork Identifier free?

You can download it and try it free. Premium is a weekly subscription with a 3-day free trial; cancel any time in your store settings.

Does it work in museums?

Yes. Many museums allow photos without flash; the Sistine Chapel and some exhibitions don't, so check the signs.

Can it identify paintings I own?

It can describe the style, likely period and similar artists, and identify well-known works and prints. It can't confirm whether a painting is an original or estimate its value reliably — for that, see our guide on finding out if a painting is valuable.

Which languages does it support?

English, Turkish, French, German and Spanish.

Is it on Android?

Yes, on Google Play, and on the App Store for iPhone and iPad.
