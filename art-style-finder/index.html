How it works

FAQ

Can a painting belong to more than one style?

Yes. Many artists moved between styles, and movements overlap in time. That's why the finder shows three suggestions.

How do I find the exact artist?

Use an artwork identifier app or reverse image search, then check the signature and the back of the painting.
